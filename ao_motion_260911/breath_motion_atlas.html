What the four columns are

now is the loop as it ran on 2026-09-13: 512×256 at 58.3 Hz, measured effective delay 1.46 frames = 25.0 ms. +gx extrapolates the disturbance instead of the residual, taking the delay to one frame. 110Hz and 122Hz add 128 lines, the second with the flyback at its 126 µs floor.

What 128 lines costs

Either 2:1 pixels (0.946×1.89 µm, keeping the 484×242 µm field) or a 484×121 µm field (keeping square pixels). Same frame rate either way — it depends only on line count, not on scan amplitude.

Why prediction is not the answer

ceiling is the most a perfect breath-synchronised predictor could remove, set by how much breaths actually repeat. For most genotypes it sits below what feedback already achieves — only Vgat breathes regularly enough for prediction to compete.

Measured vs modelled

Rate, period, rise, period CV and wave var are measured from each recording. Every removal percentage is modelled — that recording's own spectrum pushed through the loop's transfer function. The model is anchored on the rig's measured 0.5–8 Hz sweep and is untested above it.
